Privacy Policy

App: CalculaTuVela · Last updated: July 17, 2026

The Spanish version of this policy is the original; this English translation is provided for convenience.

This Privacy Policy describes how the CalculaTuVela mobile application (the “App”) handles information when you use it. The App is a tool for candle makers to manage recipes, inventory, expenses and price calculations. By using the App you agree to the practices described in this document.

1. Information you create in the App

The content you create in the App —recipes, inventory, expenses and calculations— is stored only in your device's local storage. This data is not sent to our servers and is not shared with us or with third parties. If you uninstall the App, this content is deleted from your device.

The App does not require you to create an account and does not ask for personal data such as your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts or photos.

2. Information collected automatically

To keep the App stable and improve its quality, we use Google Firebase services that automatically collect certain technical and usage information:

  • Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports): error and crash logs, diagnostic information, device type and model, operating system version, App version and device or installation identifiers.
  • Firebase Analytics (usage analytics): App interaction events, number of sessions, device or installation identifiers and approximate location derived from the IP address (at country/region level).

This information is anonymous or pseudonymous: we do not use it to identify you personally. We use it solely to detect and fix crashes, understand which features are used and improve the experience.

3. How we use the information

  • Diagnose and fix crashes and errors.
  • Analyze the App's performance and stability.
  • Understand overall usage to prioritize improvements.

We do not use this information for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties.

4. Sharing data with third parties

The technical and usage data described above is processed by Google (Firebase) as a service provider. Google handles this data under its own privacy policy, available at https://policies.google.com/privacy. We do not share your information with any other third party.

5. Data retention

Crashlytics crash reports are kept for a limited period (approximately 90 days) in line with Firebase policies. Analytics data is kept according to the standard Firebase Analytics settings.

6. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect information. Diagnostic data is sent to Google's services over encrypted connections. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100 % secure.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Your choices and rights

  • You can delete all local content by uninstalling the App.
  • You can limit the collection of diagnostic data at the system level from your Android device settings (for example, Google's usage & diagnostics options).
  • If you have questions about your data or want to exercise your rights, you can contact us at the address below.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post any changes on this page and update the “Last updated” date.
