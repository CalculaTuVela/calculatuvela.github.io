The product

The whole workshop, in four tools

From the recipe to the final price. CalculaTuVela replaces the spreadsheet and keeps calculation, inventory and expenses in the maker's pocket.

Recipes

Save every candle with its materials, amounts and instructions, and give it its own photo from the gallery or the camera. Without asking for a single permission.

Inventory

Track wax, fragrances, wicks and containers. Know what you have and what it costs before taking the next order.

Expenses

Log purchases and indirect costs so the final price reflects what each candle truly costs to make.

Pricing calculator

Enter costs and margin and instantly get the suggested price and profit per candle. No hidden formulas, and one tap shares it over WhatsApp.

01

Multi-module clean architecture

Each feature splits into domain / data / ui, with dependency inversion via Hilt. The UI never knows the data layer.

02

Reactive state with Flow

MVVM ViewModels expose one StateFlow per screen; repositories return Flow<Result<T>> and Room persists locally.

03

Automated CI/CD & quality

Every PR runs JVM tests, Robolectric UI tests, Detekt (allRules, zero issues) and dependency ordering. Releases are shrunk with R8 down to a 2.3 MB APK, signed and published to Play from GitHub Actions.
